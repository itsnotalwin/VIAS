# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Individual people (creatives, researchers, collectors) building a personal visual archive: saving links, images, video, PDFs, documents, HTML and notes, then finding and arranging them again. (Confirmed by user: personal visual archive.)

## Product Purpose
VIAS ("vias-os") is a personal visual archive. Items are saved with thumbnails, tags, collections, notes and favorites; browsed in a grid or a free-form canvas with frames; analysed by Gemini for tags/descriptions; synced via Firebase with local IndexedDB storage. It ships as an installable PWA. Success: retrieving and arranging saved material feels effortless.

## Positioning
A visual-first archive with a spatial canvas and AI tagging, rather than a flat bookmark list. (Inferred from the codebase; unconfirmed.)

## Operating Context
Used on desktop and phone (PWA); dark and light themes exist; items include local files and web links.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Stack: React 19, Vite, Tailwind v4, motion, lucide-react, Express server, Firebase auth/Firestore, Gemini. Views: grid, canvas, detail panel, lightbox, sidebar collections, dashboard, import/export, settings, login, startup screen. Name is open to change (user: open to renaming).

## Brand Commitments
Current name VIAS (open to change). Existing logo.svg and icon are incumbent evidence, not binding.

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, customers or metrics. Existing assets in /public and /src/assets/images.

## Product Principles
- The saved material is the hero; the interface recedes.
- Retrieval beats collection: finding things must be fast.
- Spatial arrangement is a first-class way of thinking, not a gimmick.
